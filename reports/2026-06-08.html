Report Date: May 20, 2026

Revenue: $81.62 billion (+85% YoY, beat consensus of $78.42B)

Key Highlights from Management:

  • Blackwell Ramp: CEO Jensen Huang confirmed Blackwell is in "full-scale production" across hyperscalers and system makers. Demand described as "extraordinary."
  • AI Infrastructure Buildout: Huang described current AI infrastructure expansion as "the largest infrastructure expansion in human history." Hyperscaler capex for 2026 revised up to $725B.
  • Vera Rubin Launch: NVIDIA announced Vera, a standalone data center CPU targeting the $200B data center CPU market. OpenAI, Anthropic, and SpaceX named as first customers. Samples already in customer hands.
  • Capital Returns: Board authorized $80B share repurchase program. Quarterly dividend increased 25x from $0.01 to $0.25 per share.
  • Sovereign AI: Sovereign AI spending tripled to over $30B in fiscal 2026, representing a new growth vector beyond hyperscalers.

Why This Matters for Swing Trading:

This earnings report removed uncertainty and confirmed the AI infrastructure supercycle remains intact. The beat-and-raise pattern continues for the 7th consecutive quarter. Q2 guidance of $91B represents another 12% sequential increase, demonstrating that demand is not slowing.

The pullback from $236 to $204 following this strong report is technical, not fundamental. Investors took profits after a parabolic run, and the stock is now consolidating gains. This creates a high-probability entry zone for swing traders: strong fundamentals + technical pullback + clear catalyst (Q2 likely to beat again in August) = A+ setup.
